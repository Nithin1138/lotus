    Real tools when you need them

    Tools that need settings open in their own window with a live preview. Every audio and video tool has a player with a seek bar, and trimming gives you draggable start and end handles.

    Every job has its own controls

    • Pause and resume. Pause freezes the whole job, including FFmpeg, and stops the clock. Resume carries on exactly where it stopped.
    • Cancel. Stops the job and removes its unfinished files. Your originals are never touched.
    • Hide. Tucks the card away while the job keeps running, and a tray notification tells you when it finishes.
    • Two at a time. Two jobs run in parallel and the rest wait their turn. Long encodes are never cut off.

    Your files stay on your machine

    Online converters ask you to upload your confidential photos, contracts and recordings to someone else's server. DropDial does 100% of the work on your own hardware.

    • Nothing is uploaded. Every conversion runs locally and works completely offline.
    • Originals are never overwritten. Outputs get a clear suffix, and duplicates get a number.
    • Everything is included. Native system engines, Apple Vision OCR, FFmpeg and Python ship inside.
    • Zero background footprint. Stays tucked in the menu bar / system tray. Under 28 MB RAM.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How does the 7-day free trial work?

    You get unrestricted access to all format conversions, the Actions Dial, batch operations, video trimming, and document tools for 7 days. No credit card or account required. After 7 days, you can purchase a lifetime license key to continue using DropDial.

    How does the DropDial Pro license work?

    DropDial Pro is a single one-time payment of $19 / ₹999 with zero subscriptions or recurring fees. One license can be activated on up to 3 personal devices across macOS and Windows, and includes all future updates.

    Does license activation require internet?

    No. DropDial uses offline public-key cryptography (ECDSA P-256). When you enter your key, it is verified 100% locally on your machine with zero server pings or telemetry.

    Are my files uploaded anywhere?

    Never. Everything is processed on your computer by native system engines. DropDial works seamlessly in air-gapped environments without any internet connection.

    What is the Actions Dial?

    The Actions Dial gives you instant universal shortcuts at your cursor. Hold ⌃ Control (macOS) or Alt (Windows) while dragging any file, or press F10 to AirDrop, copy paths, move files, strip EXIF metadata, float files to shelf, or jump straight into Terminal.

    Where do the converted files go?

    Into the same folder as the original, with a suffix such as photo-converted.jpg. If that name exists, a number is appended. Your original files are never altered or overwritten.

    Do I need Microsoft Office?

    Only for layout-exact exports of Word, Excel, and PowerPoint files into PDF or images. DropDial drives your installed Office suite for 100% pixel-perfect fidelity. Without Office, you can still extract raw text and data.

    How do I update or uninstall it?

    Quit DropDial from the menu bar / system tray. To update, simply replace the application with the latest release. To uninstall, delete the application. DropDial leaves no registry clutter or startup hooks.
