# DropDial Pricing Strategy & Recommendations

## 1. What Buyers Said (Van Westendorp Analysis)

From 100 simulated panel buyer interviews:

- **Point of Marginal Cheapness (PMC):** **$7.05** (below this, buyers assume low quality or spyware).
- **Optimal Price Point (OPP):** **$7.10** (minimum friction point).
- **Indifference Price Point (IPP):** **$15.08** (point where equal proportions perceive it as a bargain vs. expensive).
- **Point of Marginal Expensiveness (PME):** **$33.14** (above this, rejection spikes dramatically).
- **Acceptable Price Range:** **$7.05 to $33.14**.

## 2. What Competitors Charge

- **Permute 3 (macOS only):** $14.99 one-time.
- **CleanShot X (macOS screenshot utility):** $29.00 one-time.
- **CloudConvert / Smallpdf:** $9.00 – $25.00 / month ($108 – $300 / year).
- **FileConverter (Windows):** Free (open source, unmaintained).

## 3. What the Business Needs (Unit Economics)

- **Variable cost per license:** $1.40 (Stripe fees + auth CDN + support buffer).
- **Fixed monthly overhead:** $100/mo (Edge CDN + cert amortization + ops).
- **Contribution margin at $19:** $17.60 (93%).
- **Break-even:** Just **1 license per day** covers all server and administrative overheads.
