# DropDial

DropDial is a native, offline desktop utility for macOS and Windows that lives silently in your menu bar or system tray. Whenever you need to convert, crop, compress, redact, OCR, or transform a file, simply drag it from Finder or Explorer directly onto the dial or use a global shortcut. 

It handles 5 core media categories: Images, Videos, Audio, PDFs, and Archives. Everything runs 100% locally on your computer—zero cloud uploads, zero latency, and zero data leakage.
