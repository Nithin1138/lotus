# DropDial Board Brief

## The Business
- **Product:** DropDial, a native desktop media utility for macOS and Windows that converts, compresses, edits, OCRs, and transforms media files (Images, Videos, Audio, PDFs, and Archives) directly from the menu bar / system tray via an instant radial dial at the mouse cursor.
- **Operating Model:** 100% offline, privacy-first, zero telemetry or cloud uploads. Runs on native hardware (Apple Silicon VideoToolbox, Apple Vision OCR, Windows Media Foundation, PyMuPDF, FFmpeg).
- **Pricing & Offer:** $19 one-time lifetime license for 3 devices across Mac and Windows with 1 year of updates; $14 launch special; 14-day money-back guarantee; includes bonuses (CLI/Raycast extension, batch designer presets, PDF redaction).
- **Unit Economics:** $19 price, $1.40 variable cost (93% contribution margin), $100/mo fixed costs. Break-even is 1 license per day. Projected Year 1 profit: $60,000 on 10 units/day.
- **Validation:** 100-buyer simulated panel showed a 66% buy rate, led by 100% buy rate in Privacy-conscious professionals and 95% in Anti-subscription pragmatists. Acceptable price range from Van Westendorp sensitivity: $7.05 to $33.14 (bargain sweet spot: $15.08).
- **Distribution:** Direct web sales (dropdial.app), Stripe/Razorpay payments, automated Ed25519 cryptographic license keys, viral Product Hunt & Show HN launch.
